How we work

What you can hold us to.

Anyone can promise good delivery. We would rather tell you specifically what to expect, and let you judge us against it.

Our commitments

Four things we will not compromise on.

These are not marketing lines. They are the standard we ask you to measure every engagement against, from a first phone call through to the last invoice.

We listen before we pitch

Every engagement starts with discovery. We will not propose a solution before we understand your operations, your people, and what you are actually trying to fix. If we recommend something in the first conversation, that is a red flag, not a good sign.

We speak your language

No talk of large language models, transformers, or agents unless you want to go there. If we cannot explain something in plain English, we do not understand it well enough yet to be charging you for it.

We build working things

There is a difference between a consultancy that hands you a strategy deck and one that ships. The AI feature on our own site is ours, built by us, running live in production, not a mock-up for a sales call.

We are honest about limitations

AI cannot solve everything. We will flag the risks, name the things that will not work, and tell you when the answer is no, even when saying yes would be more profitable for us in the short term.

Honestly

What AI will not do for you.

Every consultancy will tell you what AI can do. Fewer will tell you where it falls down, or when you should not buy it at all. Here is where we will push back, every time.

AI will not fix a broken process

Automating a process that does not work simply produces bad output faster. If we find the real problem is the process, we will tell you, and fixing that may not need AI at all.

Sometimes the honest answer is no

Most UK businesses cite no identified need as their main barrier to AI, and sometimes they are right. If an assessment finds AI is not where your money should go this year, that is what the report will say.

Off-the-shelf tools often win

A lot of what a small business needs is a well-configured tool and an hour of training, not a bespoke build. We will recommend that route when it is the right one, even though it earns us less.
