Privacy Policy

How Velyntic collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: July 2026. Velyntic is a trading name of Michael Goggs, registered in England. ICO registration: pending.

Who we are

Velyntic is an AI adoption consultancy and web design studio based in North Yorkshire, UK. When we refer to "we", "us", or "our" in this policy, we mean Velyntic. When we refer to "you" or "your", we mean anyone who visits our website, uses our services, or submits information through our forms.

What data we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to your enquiry or deliver a service. This includes:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number (when you provide them via a contact or intake form)
  • Your business name, industry, and any project details you share through our intake forms
  • Technical data collected automatically when you visit our site: your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on the site
  • Cookies: we use essential cookies only. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics that follow you around the internet

How we use your data

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Scope and deliver the service you have engaged us for
  • Send you project updates during an active engagement
  • Improve our website and services based on aggregate, anonymised usage data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We will never do this.

Third-party services

Our website uses a small number of third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: provides hosting, DNS, and security for our website. Cloudflare may process your IP address and technical data to deliver the site and protect against threats. Their privacy policy is available at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Basin: processes form submissions from our contact and intake forms. Basin stores your form data securely and forwards it to our inbox. Their privacy policy is available at usebasin.com/privacy.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typefaces used on our website. Google may collect your IP address when fonts are loaded. Their privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

How we store your data

Form submissions are processed by Basin and delivered to our email inbox. We store project-related data in secure, access-controlled systems for the duration of the engagement and for a reasonable period afterwards (typically 24 months) for reference and support purposes. After this period, we delete it unless you ask us to keep it or there is a legal reason to retain it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (where there is no legal obligation to retain it)
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@velyntic.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the "last updated" date revised accordingly. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at support@velyntic.com.
